About Mendo

Mendo is an AI-powered over-the-counter (OTC) medicine vending system. It uses natural language processing to understand your symptoms and recommend safe, age-appropriate medicines.

Describe your symptoms in English, Tagalog, Bisaya, or a mix (Taglish) and the system will match them to appropriate OTC medication available in the vending machine.

This system is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Always consult a healthcare provider for serious or persistent symptoms.

Why do we ask your age?

Some over-the-counter medicines have minimum age requirements. For example, certain pain relievers are not recommended for children under 12.

By entering your age, we automatically filter out any medicines that are not safe for your age group. This ensures every recommendation is appropriate for you.

Supported Languages

Our AI understands multiple Philippine languages. You can describe your symptoms in any of the following:

English — "I have a headache and cough"
Tagalog — "Masakit ulo ko, may ubo"
Bisaya — "Sakit akong ulo, naay ubo"
Taglish — Mix of any of the above

Voice input also supports these languages. Just tap the microphone and speak naturally.
